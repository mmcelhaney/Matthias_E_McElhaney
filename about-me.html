My name is Matthias Ezekiel McElhaney.

I was almost born in the early 2000s and have always been fascinated by the intersection of technology and art.

Through this website, I aim to share my journey, connect with like-minded individuals, and explore opportunities in both the tech and art worlds.

Feel free to explore the different sections of this website to learn more about my interests, projects, and vision for the future.

I am generally interested in all kinds of science, but I love building things with my hands. I think that may be pushing me in the direction of Mechanical Engineering.

In the future, I hope to combine my interests in technology, art, and hands-on building to create innovative projects that make a positive impact.

I also love sequential art, such as comics, manga, and graphic novels. As well as western animation and Anime.

I have strong opinions about how AI has influenced both the tech and art worlds, and I am keen to explore its potential while being mindful of its ethical implications.

Thank you for visiting my website!

I welcome any feedback, collaboration, or opportunities to engage with others who share my passions.
